# Hospital ER Visits Dashboard

This Tableau dashboard explores patient visits to the Emergency Room (ER) between **April 2019 and October 2020**. The goal is to uncover trends and insights related to wait times, patient satisfaction, demographics, and ER usage patterns.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard presents a collection of interactive visualizations built in Tableau to analyze:

- **Total Number of ER Patients** during the dataset's time frame.
- **Gender Breakdown** of ER visitors, including Female, Male, and Non-conforming identities.
- **Average Patient Satisfaction Score** (scale of 0–10) over time.
- **Average Patient Wait Time (in minutes)** over time.
- **Aggregated Averages**:
  - Total Patient Satisfaction Score
  - Total Patient Wait Time
- **Patient Count by Age Group**:
  - 0–18 years
  - 19–34 years
  - 35–59 years
  - 60+ years
- **Hourly Wait Time Patterns**:
  - Average patient wait time for every hour of every weekday
- **Patient Distribution by Race**
- **Number of Department Referrals**

>  **Note**: The "Patient Flag Time" column from the original dataset was removed due to lack of clarity around its meaning.
